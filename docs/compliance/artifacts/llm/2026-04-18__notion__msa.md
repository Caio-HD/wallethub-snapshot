# Notion Master Subscription Agreement

**Last Updated: April 2, 2026**

Notion has updated its Master Subscription Agreement effective April 2, 2026. For existing Customers, these updates will apply beginning on May 1, 2026. For new Customers, these updates apply immediately.

This Agreement sets forth the terms and conditions between Customer and Notion Labs, Inc. (“Notion,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) which govern Customer’s and its Affiliates’ access and use of Notion’s Services. The Agreement is effective as of the date that you sign up for any Service online or submit an Order Form that references this Agreement (the “Effective Date”).

## Definitions
- **“Account Information”** means information about your Notion account, including names, usernames, passwords, phone numbers, email addresses, and usage information.
- **“Customer Data”** means all data stored by or on behalf of Customer in the Services.
- **“Services”** means Notion’s online software-as-a-service platform, including APIs and related applications.
- **“Usage Data”** means information relating to the performance and use of the Services.

## 1. Services
- Notion grants a worldwide, non-exclusive, non-transferable right to use the Services during the Subscription Term.
- Subscriptions automatically renew unless notified at least 30 days prior.
- Technical support is typically provided 24/7 via electronic mail.

## 2. Use of the Services
- **Restrictions:** Users may not reverse engineer, sell, or use the Services to build a competitive product.
- **Responsibility:** Customer is responsible for all actions by its Users and for maintaining account security.
- **Excess Usage:** Notion may require upgrades if usage limits are exceeded.

## 3. Ownership
- **Customer Data:** Customer retains all ownership rights. Notion is granted a limited license to host and process data to provide the Services.
- **Notion Ownership:** Notion retains all rights to the Services, Documentation, and Usage Data.
- **Feedback:** Any feedback provided becomes the property of Notion.

## 4. Fees and Taxes
- Fees are specified in the Order Form and are generally non-refundable.
- Downgrades during a Subscription Term are prohibited.
- Late payments may incur interest (1.5% per month).

## 5. Warranties
- **Mutual:** Both parties represent they have the authority to enter the agreement.
- **Notion Warranty:** The Services will function substantially in accordance with the Documentation.
- **Disclaimer:** All other warranties, including merchantability and fitness for a particular purpose, are disclaimed.

## 6. Confidentiality
- Parties must protect each other's Confidential Information and use it only as permitted under the Agreement.

## 7. Privacy and Security
- Notion maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards.
- The Data Processing Addendum (DPA) is incorporated into the Agreement.

## 8. Indemnification
- Notion will defend claims of intellectual property infringement.
- Customer will defend claims arising from their use of the Service or Customer Data.

## 9. Limitation of Liability
- Liability is generally capped at the fees paid during the 12 months preceding the claim.
- Consequential and incidental damages are excluded.

## 10. Term and Termination
- The agreement continues as long as there is an active subscription.
- Termination for breach is allowed with a 30-day cure period.
- Customer Data is available for retrieval for 30 days post-termination.

## 11. Beta Services and Free Trials
- Beta Services are provided "as-is" without warranty.
- Free trials are for evaluation and can be terminated by Notion at any time.

## 12. Trade Controls
- Services are subject to U.S. export controls and sanctions. Prohibited persons or countries (e.g., Cuba, Iran, North Korea) may not access the Service.

## 13. General
- Governed by the laws of California, with venue in San Francisco.
- Notices must be in writing (English).
- Assignment is restricted without prior consent.
- The Agreement constitutes the complete and exclusive understanding between the parties.
